Internal services (reachable from app servers only):
  10.0.0.5     - metrics.internal          (Prometheus)
  10.0.0.8     - metadata.internal:8080    (legacy config service, no auth —
                                             on the deprecation list)
  127.0.0.1    - localhost (this host)     (portal app itself runs on :5001)

Reminders:
  - The portal app server can reach the above hosts even though your
    laptop can't — if you need to sanity-check one of them, open a
    ticket with #platform-team rather than trying to reach it directly.
  - metadata.internal has no auth in front of it on purpose (legacy),
    do NOT expose it externally. It's getting replaced this quarter.

Known external integrations:
  - Webhook previews go out through the portal's Webhook Tool —
    outbound only, no allow-list configured yet (also on the backlog).
