New Hire Onboarding Checklist
==============================

Week 1
  [x] Get portal login from your manager
  [x] Read handbook.txt
  [ ] Set up your profile (Dashboard > Your profile)
  [ ] Introduce yourself in Team Notes
  [ ] Review network-topology.txt if you're on Platform/Infra

Week 2
  [ ] Shadow a teammate on an on-call rotation
  [ ] Request access to your team's shared drive (ask your manager —
      not self-service yet)
  [ ] Complete the security awareness training (link on the intranet
      home page, not in this portal)

Notes from your onboarding buddy
---------------------------------
"Honestly the portal is pretty barebones right now — IT knows, it's on
the roadmap. If something looks broken, it's probably just not built
yet, not you doing anything wrong. Ping #it-helpdesk on chat if you
get stuck."

FAQ
---
Q: Why can't I see other departments' notes?
A: Everyone shares the same Team Notes board for now — there's no
   per-department separation yet.

Q: Who do I contact about a backup/restore?
A: Settings > Import on your own account, or #platform-team if it's a
   bigger restore than a single workspace.
